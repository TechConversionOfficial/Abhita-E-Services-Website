## About the business

- **What it does:** business verification as a service. Approved businesses run identity and KYC checks (PAN, Aadhaar via DigiLocker, phone intelligence), business checks (GST, company CIN, employee verification), vehicle checks (VAHAN, FASTag, RC lookups) and report services (credit, ITR, health verification) from one account, with downloadable reports.
- **Who it sells to:** compliance, onboarding and operations teams in insurance, BFSI, logistics, healthcare, retail and e-commerce, manufacturing, staffing, telecom and education.
- **How access works:** the platform is approval-gated. A business registers, is verified and approved, then receives 20 complimentary credits usable across the services enabled for it. Sensitive services are restricted to verified, licensed businesses.

## The problem

A regulated, credit-based verification product is hard to explain on a page. Each service has its own rules, some are restricted, and the buyer has to understand what they can run, what it costs in credits and how to get approved, before they will register. The website had to carry that explanation clearly enough that a compliance team can self-qualify, and then take the registration without a sales call.

## How Tech Conversion approached it

1. **Separate the explanation from the product.** The marketing site was built to explain, rank and convert; the verification portal itself (login, credit wallet, verification requests, reports) runs as a separate application, so neither constrains the other.
2. **One page per verification service.** PAN, GST, Aadhaar, company and vehicle verification each got a landing page written for the team that searches for it, which is also what lets the site rank for each service on its own.
3. **Make the access model plain.** Registration, approval, complimentary credits and the shared credit wallet are explained before the sign-up, so the businesses that register are the ones that qualify.
4. **Build search in, not on.** Headings, structured data and metadata were written with the pages.
5. **Hand over completely.** Code, hosting, domain and every account belong to the client, in their own name.

## Standards the build follows

- [Core Web Vitals](https://web.dev/articles/vitals): loading, interactivity and visual stability measured on a mid-range phone.
- [Google Search Essentials](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials): the requirements a page has to meet to appear in search.
- [schema.org structured data](https://schema.org/): organisation, breadcrumb and page-type markup in the templates.
- [Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023](https://www.meity.gov.in/data-protection-framework): what the forms collect and how consent is recorded, which matters more than usual on a site about verification data.
